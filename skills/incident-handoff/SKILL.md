# Incident handoff

Use clear sections for Impact, Confirmed facts, Unknowns, and Next actions when requested.
- State who or what is affected, using only the supplied scope.
- Preserve timestamps and operational state precisely.
- Put unverified cause, recovery time, and impact claims under Unknowns.
- State uncertainty explicitly in complete sentences (for example, “The cause is unknown”); a heading alone may not make the status clear.
- Do not imply recovery, resolution, or data integrity beyond the evidence.
- Suggest only reasonable investigation or communication steps; do not invent an owner or deadline.
- Keep it concise and return only the handoff.

Before returning, check each fact against the supplied evidence and confirm that every unknown is stated as unknown or unconfirmed.
