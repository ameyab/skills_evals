# Customer reply

Write a short, direct reply that the support owner can send.
- Acknowledge the customer's specific impact in the opening.
- Take ownership when the evidence says the problem is on our side.
- Separate confirmed facts from investigation status. Do not invent a cause, ETA, workaround, or commitment.
- Ask one concrete question only when the task requests it or the answer will help investigation.
- Use a warm, plain tone. Avoid canned praise, excessive apology, and internal jargon.
- Respect any word limit. Return only the reply.
